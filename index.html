Control points

Parameter space

Drag the pink dot. Lines are knots. The pink dot on the surface is S(u, v); control points with a cyan ring influence it.

Partition of unity

The basis functions Ni,3(u) and Nj,3(v). At every parameter value only 4 of them are not 0 (thick), and their values always add up to 1 (bar Σ). Drag in a plot to move u or v. Weights do not change these curves.

Formulas

A surface point is a weighted average of the control points Pi,j:

S(u,v)= ∑i=0n ∑j=0m Ri,j(u,v) Pi,j

The share Ri,j of a control point comes from the basis functions and its weight wi,j:

Ri,j(u,v)= Ni,p(u) Nj,q(v) wi,j ∑k=0n ∑l=0m Nk,p(u) Nl,q(v) wk,l

ui are the knots. A fraction with denominator 0 counts as 0.
